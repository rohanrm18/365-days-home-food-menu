# Dish photos checklist

The app shows a real photo for a dish when a file with the name below exists in `assets/meals/` (`.jpg` or `.png`). Dishes without a file show a coloured tile, so you can add photos a few at a time.

## How to get free photos

1. **Free AI image tools** (for example Bing Image Creator, Google Gemini or ChatGPT free plans): paste the prompt from the table, download the picture and rename it to the file name.

2. **Free stock sites** (Wikimedia Commons, Pexels, Pixabay, Unsplash): use the search link, pick a photo that really shows that dish, check its licence allows reuse (Wikimedia Commons CC-BY and CC-BY-SA photos need credit to the photographer) and rename it.

Tips: landscape 16:9 pictures about 800 px wide and under 300 KB look best. Upload many files at once on GitHub: open the repo, go to `assets/meals`, then **Add file > Upload files**, drag the pictures in and commit. The builds then include them.
